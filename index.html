Who we are

Aircond solutions for homes and businesses across Kuala Lumpur

Ad Aircond Solution is an air conditioning service company working out of Titiwangsa Central in Kuala Lumpur. We cover wall-mounted splits in condos and terrace houses, and the multi-unit systems running in offices, clinics and retail lots.

Most jobs reach us the same way: a unit that no longer cools the way it did. We check airflow, drainage, the electrical side and the refrigerant circuit before recommending anything, so the work you pay for addresses the actual fault instead of replacing parts on a guess.

Our approach

We handle aircond problems from diagnosis to repair

A unit that is not cooling can point to several different things — a dirty coil, a failing capacitor, a blocked drain, a refrigerant leak or a control fault. Each one needs a different fix, so the inspection comes first and the recommendation follows from what we find.

  • Experienced techniciansInspection, servicing and repair carried out by people who work on these systems daily.
  • Practical recommendationsAdvice matched to the condition of your system and how the space is actually used.
  • Quality workmanshipCareful work at the unit, and care taken with the room and finishes around it.
